# Kind vs wicked loop

**Each column asks whether one link holds in the loop that turns experience into skill, so an empty cell is a place the loop could break that no axis names.**

## The columns

A setting is kind when you can learn from experience and then act on what you learnt. That needs five links to hold. Break any one and the setting turns wicked.

| Column | The question | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Past | Does the past predict? Do past cases tell you how the next one will go? | [Conditions for intuitive expertise](../frameworks/2009-conditions-for-intuitive-expertise.md) (a setting regular enough to learn) and [Kind vs wicked learning environments](../frameworks/2001-kind-vs-wicked-learning-environments.md) (the world has moved on since you learnt) |
| State | Can you see the state? Can you tell what situation you are in right now? | [Conditions for intuitive expertise](../frameworks/2009-conditions-for-intuitive-expertise.md) (cues the expert can read, like the fire lieutenant's hot, quiet room) and [Task environment properties](../frameworks/1995-task-environment-properties.md) (can the agent see everything that matters?) |
| Check | Can you check the answer? Can you tell whether you were right, and soon? | [Kind vs wicked learning environments](../frameworks/2001-kind-vs-wicked-learning-environments.md) and [Conditions for intuitive expertise](../frameworks/2009-conditions-for-intuitive-expertise.md) (quick, clear, honest feedback) |
| Retry | Can you try again? Do you get more goes, so a miss teaches rather than ends things? | [Conditions for intuitive expertise](../frameworks/2009-conditions-for-intuitive-expertise.md) (long practice) and [One-way vs two-way doors](../frameworks/2015-one-way-vs-two-way-doors.md) (decide fast and learn from the result when you can undo it) |
| Acting | Does acting change it? Does your own move, forecast or score change the thing you are learning about? | [Kind vs wicked learning environments](../frameworks/2001-kind-vs-wicked-learning-environments.md) (the typhoid doctor) and [Reflexivity](../frameworks/1987-reflexivity.md) (beliefs move the facts they are about) |

## Where each axis goes

| Axis | Cell | Why |
|---|---|---|

| [Coherent vs incoherent runs](../axes/coherent-vs-incoherent-runs.md) | Environment, Past | The pooled record of past runs describes none of them, so it can't tell you how this run goes. |
| [Clustered vs scattered runs](../axes/clustered-vs-scattered-runs.md) | Environment, State | With no clear types there are no clues to tell which run you are in. |
| [Thin vs fat tails](../axes/thin-vs-fat-tails.md) | Environment, Past | The past average hides the one extreme case that outweighs the rest. |
| [Stable vs chaotic](../axes/stable-vs-chaotic.md) | Environment, Past | A past case that started almost the same soon ends up somewhere else. |
| [Durable vs perishable lessons](../axes/durable-vs-perishable-lessons.md) | Environment, Past | The rules behind past data change, so what the past taught goes stale. |
| [Turn-based vs real-time](../axes/turn-based-vs-real-time.md) | Environment, State | What you read a minute ago may no longer be the situation you act in. |
| [Self-righting vs tipping](../axes/self-righting-vs-tipping.md) | Environment, Retry | Past the edge the system flips and won't come back, so a miss ends the old world. |

## What it shows

### Empty cells

| Cell | Candidate axis | The question | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Information, Acting | Unobtrusive vs obtrusive looking | Does looking at the thing change what you see? | Already an axis: [Inert vs reflexive](../axes/inert-vs-reflexive.md) ("a rock doesn't care if you measure it") |
| Actions, Past | Portable vs context-bound moves | Does a move that worked in past cases work again here? California copied Tennessee's small classes in 1996 and got far less from them. | Untested |
| Actions, State | Confirmed vs unconfirmed moves | Can you tell whether your move actually took effect? | Already an axis: [Immediate vs delayed feedback](../axes/immediate-vs-delayed-feedback.md), whose hard end says the result may come late or never |
| Performance, Past | Fixed vs moving goal | Does what counted as good last time still count now? | [Already tested](../CONTRIBUTING.md#is-it-a-new-axis) |
| Performance, State | Visible vs hidden standing | Can you tell how you stand against the goal right now? | Already an axis: [Immediate vs delayed feedback](../axes/immediate-vs-delayed-feedback.md) |
| Performance, Acting | Ungameable vs gameable score | Does chasing the score change what it measures? | Already an axis: [Direct vs proxy measure](../axes/direct-vs-proxy-measure.md) and [Neutral vs adversarial](../axes/neutral-vs-adversarial.md), both fed by Goodhart's law |
| Agent, Check | Calibrated vs uncalibrated solver | Can the solver tell when its own judgement is right? | Untested |
| Agent, Retry | Practised vs unpractised solver | Has this solver had many goes at this kind of task? | Already an axis: [Familiar vs novel to the solver](../axes/familiar-vs-novel-to-the-solver.md) |
| Agent, Acting | Detached vs entangled solver | Does the solver's own presence change the case, as the typhoid doctor's hands did? | Already an axis: [Inert vs reflexive](../axes/inert-vs-reflexive.md) |

Two leads are untested. Portable vs context-bound moves sits close to [Durable vs perishable lessons](../axes/durable-vs-perishable-lessons.md), which asks about drift over time rather than across places. Confirmed vs unconfirmed moves also lives as an action under the malleable middle of [Self-righting vs tipping](../axes/self-righting-vs-tipping.md), the Three Mile Island valve. Calibrated vs uncalibrated solver comes straight from Kahneman and Klein, who found that confidence is a poor guide to skill.

### Overfull cells

- **Environment, Past (6).** Split candidate. Stochastic, incoherent runs, fat tails, chaos, perishable lessons and wear all break prediction, but for six different reasons, so "does the past predict?" is too coarse to sort them.
- **Information, Past (3).** Fine as it is. Knightian uncertainty, unforeseeable outcomes and scarce data all say there are too few past cases, but they differ on whether the odds, the list of outcomes or the data are missing.
- **Information, State (3).** Fine as it is. Unknown unknowns, asymmetric information and a low base rate each hide the state in a different way: you don't know what to ask, someone hides it, or the clue misleads.
- **Information, Check (3).** Merge review. Observational, unverifiable and delayed feedback are all "you can't tell whether you were right". Hogarth counts the road not taken and the late grade as separate parts of wicked, and a cheap check before you act is a third thing, so they can stay apart.

### Straddles and axes with no home

Three axes straddle two cells.

- [Deterministic vs stochastic](../axes/deterministic-vs-stochastic.md) asks both whether the past predicts a single case and whether one result is a fair grade.
- [Rehearsable vs live only](../axes/rehearsable-vs-live-only.md) asks both whether you get a practice go and whether practice predicts the real thing.
- [Standalone vs sequential](../axes/standalone-vs-sequential.md) asks both whether your move changes the next situation and whether you can tell which move worked.

Twenty axes have no home. They are [Reductionist vs holistic](../axes/reductionist-vs-holistic.md), [Forgiving vs exacting](../axes/forgiving-vs-exacting.md), [Tractable vs intractable options](../axes/tractable-vs-intractable-options.md), [Reducible vs irreducible uncertainty](../axes/reducible-vs-irreducible-uncertainty.md), [Transparent vs opaque causes](../axes/transparent-vs-opaque-causes.md), [Given vs invented options](../axes/given-vs-invented-options.md), [Yours to fix vs needs others to change](../axes/yours-to-fix-vs-needs-others-to-change.md), [Ample time vs time pressure](../axes/ample-time-vs-time-pressure.md), [Well vs ill-structured](../axes/well-vs-ill-structured.md), [Convergent vs divergent](../axes/convergent-vs-divergent.md), [Single objective vs trade-offs](../axes/single-objective-vs-trade-offs.md), [Shared vs contested values](../axes/shared-vs-contested-values.md), [Satisficing vs optimising](../axes/satisficing-vs-optimising.md), [Curable vs chronic](../axes/curable-vs-chronic.md), [Equal vs unequal error costs](../axes/equal-vs-unequal-error-costs.md), [Winnable vs no-win](../axes/winnable-vs-no-win.md), [Explicit vs tacit know-how](../axes/explicit-vs-tacit-know-how.md), [Bounded vs open-ended skill](../axes/bounded-vs-open-ended-skill.md), [Skin in the game vs none](../axes/skin-in-the-game-vs-none.md) and [Private vs accountable](../axes/private-vs-accountable.md). Eight of them are Performance axes. They ask what counts as good or what a miss costs, and the loop takes the goal as given.

One no home is a finding in itself. The log folded full vs partial observability into [Reducible vs irreducible uncertainty](../axes/reducible-vs-irreducible-uncertainty.md) on 2026-10-14, as its easy end. So the State column's hard end, a hidden state, is that axis's easy end, and the axis has nowhere to go.

### Axes outside their group

None. Every placed axis landed in its own group's row. The columns ask which link of the loop breaks, not where the trouble lives, so they never pull an axis across rows. The nearest call was [Rehearsable vs live only](../axes/rehearsable-vs-live-only.md), whose neighbours in Retry are the one-shot moves in Actions. That makes this grid useless for testing the groups.

## Verdict

It fails as a full test. Twenty of 50 axes have no home, far more than a grid can afford, and nearly all are about goals, stakes and who decides. The loop sees one half of the set well, the half about learning, and is blind to the other. Straddles are few at three, and the empty cells read as real questions, though seven of the nine are already asked by an axis or were tested.

What it sees that the groups don't is how the learning axes cluster by link. Ten axes say the past doesn't predict and four say you can't check whether you were right. The groups scatter the first ten across six sets in three groups, from Randomness to Know-how. It also finds two leads: whether a move carries across places, and whether the solver knows when its own judgement is right.
