# The world

* [Deterministic vs statistical](deterministic-vs-statistical.md) - Do the same inputs always give the same result, or only a stable spread of results?
* [Thin vs fat tails](thin-vs-fat-tails.md) - When results are statistical, how extreme can a single one get?
* [Smooth vs rugged](smooth-vs-rugged.md) - Can small steps carry you to the best answer, or will they get stuck?
* [Long vs short predictability horizon](long-vs-short-predictability-horizon.md) - How far ahead does a forecast hold before small errors blow up?
* [Stationary vs drifting](stationary-vs-drifting.md) - Do the lessons you learn today still hold next year?
* [Inert vs reflexive](inert-vs-reflexive.md) - Does looking at the system, or acting on your forecast, change it?
* [Neutral vs adversarial](neutral-vs-adversarial.md) - Is someone working against you?
* [Decomposable vs holistic](decomposable-vs-holistic.md) - Can you split the problem into parts, solve each one and put them back together?
* [Loose vs tight coupling](loose-vs-tight-coupling.md) - When one part fails, how fast does it spread?
* [Tractable vs intractable search](tractable-vs-intractable-search.md) - How many options do you have to search through?
* [Discrete vs continuous](discrete-vs-continuous.md) - Does the problem move in clean steps or by degrees?

# The knower

* [Known vs unknown odds](known-vs-unknown-odds.md) - Do you know the chances, or only that things are uncertain?
* [Reducible vs irreducible uncertainty](reducible-vs-irreducible-uncertainty.md) - Could more information shrink your doubt, or would no amount of fact finding settle it?
* [Small vs large world](small-vs-large-world.md) - Can you list every way things might turn out before you choose?
* [Full vs partial observability](full-vs-partial-observability.md) - Can you see everything that matters?
* [Transparent vs opaque causes](transparent-vs-opaque-causes.md) - Can you trace the chain from cause to effect?
* [Test vs watch](test-vs-watch.md) - Can you run a fair test, or can you only watch what happens?
* [Kind vs wicked feedback](kind-vs-wicked-feedback.md) - Do you find out quickly and honestly whether you were right?
* [Plentiful vs scarce data](plentiful-vs-scarce-data.md) - Do you have enough cases to learn from?
* [Explicit vs tacit know-how](explicit-vs-tacit-know-how.md) - Can the expertise be written down?
* [Familiar vs novel to the solver](familiar-vs-novel-to-the-solver.md) - Has the solver seen this kind of problem before?
* [General vs local knowledge](general-vs-local-knowledge.md) - Does the answer rest on facts anyone could look up, or on facts about this place, this firm or this moment?

# The decision

* [Well-posed vs ill-posed](well-posed-vs-ill-posed.md) - Is the goal clear and do you know when you are done?
* [Choose vs design the options](choose-vs-design-the-options.md) - Are the options handed to you, or do you have to invent them?
* [Convergent vs divergent](convergent-vs-divergent.md) - Is there one right answer, or many good ones?
* [Single vs multiple objectives](single-vs-multiple-objectives.md) - Is there one thing to get right, or several that pull against each other?
* [Shared vs contested values](shared-vs-contested-values.md) - Does everyone agree what a good result looks like?
* [Commensurable vs incommensurable values](commensurable-vs-incommensurable-values.md) - Can every want be priced in one currency?
* [Yours to fix vs needs others to change](yours-to-fix-vs-needs-others-to-change.md) - Do you hold the levers, or does the fix depend on other people changing?
* [Satisficing vs optimising](satisficing-vs-optimising.md) - Is good enough fine, or does the last bit matter?
* [Easy vs hard to verify](easy-vs-hard-to-verify.md) - Can you check an answer cheaply, even if finding it was hard?
* [Complete vs incomplete goal](complete-vs-incomplete-goal.md) - Does the score capture everything you want?
* [One-shot vs sequential](one-shot-vs-sequential.md) - Is it a single choice, or a chain where each step shapes the next?
* [Reversible vs irreversible](reversible-vs-irreversible.md) - Can you undo it if it goes wrong?
* [Survivable vs ruinous](survivable-vs-ruinous.md) - Could one bad outcome take you out of the game for good?
* [Additive vs multiplicative](additive-vs-multiplicative.md) - Does the average across many players match what one player gets over time?
* [Convex vs concave payoff](convex-vs-concave-payoff.md) - Does a swing in results help you or hurt you?
* [Even vs lopsided errors](even-vs-lopsided-errors.md) - Do false alarms and misses cost the same?
* [Slack vs time pressure](slack-vs-time-pressure.md) - How long do you have to decide, compared to how fast things change?
* [Private vs accountable](private-vs-accountable.md) - Must you explain and defend the choice?
* [Skin in the game vs none](skin-in-the-game-vs-none.md) - Does whoever makes the call pay if it goes wrong?
