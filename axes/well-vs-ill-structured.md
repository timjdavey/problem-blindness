# Well vs ill-structured

An axis in the [Goal](index.md#goal) set of the [performance](index.md#performance) group.

Is the problem clearly stated, with a known goal, known moves to get there and a clear point where you are done? Or is working out the problem, or how to attack it, part of the job? A maths exam question is well structured. "Fix homelessness" is not, so working out what the problem actually is becomes part of the job. The terms come from Herbert Simon. Don't mix them up with a "well-posed" problem in maths, which Jacques Hadamard defined as one with a single answer that moves only a little when the inputs do. That idea is closer to [Forgiving vs exacting](forgiving-vs-exacting.md).

## Actions

**Well-structured**: Solve it.
- Work it out rather than debate it. Treating a problem with a clear answer as open to endless argument means nobody sits down and solves it. [1]
- Let whoever does the work aim straight at the test, whether a new hire, a contractor or a model. With the goal, the moves and the test all fixed, search alone can get there. That is how Deep Blue beat Kasparov at chess in 1997. [1]
- Make the check mechanical. The test for done needs no judgement, so it can run on every answer. [1]

**Ill-structured**: Work out the problem before solving it.
- Draft several framings of the problem before you pick one. Each points to a different fix. Crime blamed on poverty calls for jobs, while crime blamed on weak policing calls for more officers. [3, 4]
- Treat each framing as a trial. Try one, watch how the situation responds and change it when it doesn't fit. [6]
- Pin down what you can before you plan the rest. Check which of the goal, the moves and the test is missing. The Sydney Opera House was budgeted at about A$7 million as if its plan were fixed, and it cost A$102 million. [1]
- Expect the goal to change once people see a first version, and budget for it. A client often learns what they want from the architect's first sketch, not from their own brief. [1]
- When results stay poor, question the goal as well as the method. A call centre that trains staff to keep calls short can end up with more people ringing back about problems left unfixed. [5]
- Check that the problem came before the solution. A consultant, a vendor or a model with a ready method tends to describe the problem in its own terms, so the fit only holds on paper. MD Anderson spent about $62 million on a cancer adviser built on IBM's Watson, and it was not in clinical use. [2, 6]

## Correlated with

- [Verifiable vs unverifiable](verifiable-vs-unverifiable.md) (by definition). Simon's [well-structured problems](../frameworks/1964-well-vs-ill-structured-problems.md) come with a test for an answer, and [Rittel and Webber](../frameworks/1973-tame-vs-wicked-problems.md) say a wicked problem has no point where it is done.
- [Shared vs contested values](shared-vs-contested-values.md) (bundled). [Rittel and Webber](../frameworks/1973-tame-vs-wicked-problems.md), [Checkland](../frameworks/1981-hard-vs-soft-systems.md) and [Roberts](../frameworks/2000-simple-complex-and-wicked.md) all say the hard part is that people don't agree what the problem is.
- [Single objective vs trade-offs](single-objective-vs-trade-offs.md) (bundled). Simon's house design and Jonassen's [dilemmas](../frameworks/2000-problem-typology.md) are hard to pin down because several wants pull against each other. These three axes all get bundled under "wicked", but they come apart. "Cure this cancer" has one goal that everyone shares, yet nobody can say how to get there or what to try first. Buying a car has many objectives but is well structured once you set the weights.
- [Reductionist vs holistic](reductionist-vs-holistic.md) (bundled). Ackoff's [messes](../frameworks/1974-messes.md) and Jonassen's typology put holistic problems at the ill-structured end.
- [Familiar vs novel to the solver](familiar-vs-novel-to-the-solver.md) (bundled). Simon's [non-programmed decisions](../frameworks/1960-programmed-vs-non-programmed-decisions.md) are both new and poorly structured.
- [Direct vs proxy measure](direct-vs-proxy-measure.md) (logic). If you can't state the goal, you can't score all of it. The reverse doesn't hold. Rating CVs looked well structured, yet Amazon's score left out fairness ([Need for interpretability](../frameworks/2017-need-for-interpretability.md)).
- [Given vs invented options](given-vs-invented-options.md) (independent). "Build a bridge across this river for under £10m" is well structured, and every option still has to be drawn.
- [Convergent vs divergent](convergent-vs-divergent.md) (independent). "Write a sonnet about autumn" is well structured and has endless good answers that differ in kind.
- [Curable vs chronic](curable-vs-chronic.md) (bundled). A clear end is part of what Simon means by well structured, and [Rittel and Webber](../frameworks/1973-tame-vs-wicked-problems.md) say a wicked problem has no stopping rule. But keeping a diabetic's blood sugar in range is well structured and never ends.
