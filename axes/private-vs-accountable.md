# Private vs accountable

An axis in the [Incentives](index.md#incentives) set of the [agent](index.md#agent) group.

Is the choice yours alone, or must you explain and defend it to others, such as a regulator, a court or the person affected? A hunch is fine for picking a film. A loan refusal, a court ruling or a medical decision may have to be explained, audited or defended in law, which rules out some methods even when they would work better. It can also make you think harder. That only happens if you know you will be judged before you decide, and you don't know what answer the judge wants.

## Actions

**Private choice**: Use whatever works best.
- Pick the most accurate method, even one nobody can explain. A hunch or a black box is fine when nobody needs to know why, and asking for reasons can cost accuracy for nothing. [2, 3]
- Don't explain more than you must. Having to justify a choice often just pushes you to the option that is easiest to defend. [2]
- Get a check on the choices that matter anyway. Nobody will ask you to explain, so nobody will catch a lazy call. People who expected to explain themselves to an audience whose views they didn't know thought through both sides more. [2]

**Accountable choice**: Use a method you can explain and keep the records.
- Prefer methods you can read, like a scorecard or a checklist. If you must use a hunch or a black box, be able to give a reason for each decision. [2, 3]
- Record what you knew and why you chose, at the time. Without that record people get judged on how things turned out. After New York published heart surgeons' death rates, 62% of those who replied to a survey said they had turned away a high-risk patient. [1]
- Ask who will judge the choice and what they want, before you decide. Knowing the answer an audience wants pulls you towards it. Deciding first turns the review into a defence. [2]
- Check the law first. US lenders must give a refused applicant the main reasons. Under the EU's GDPR, people have a right not to face a decision with legal effects made by a machine alone. [2, 4]

## Correlated with

- [Explicit vs tacit know-how](explicit-vs-tacit-know-how.md) (tension). Know-how you can't put into words is hard to defend in an audit, so accountability pushes towards written rules like Meehl's [formulas](../frameworks/1954-clinical-vs-statistical-prediction.md).
- [Verifiable vs unverifiable](verifiable-vs-unverifiable.md) (tension). You can only defend a choice if someone can check it.
- [Neutral vs adversarial](neutral-vs-adversarial.md) (study). [Campbell's law](../frameworks/1976-campbells-law.md) says the more a number decides people's fate, the more they bend it.
- [Shared vs contested values](shared-vs-contested-values.md) (logic). A choice that picks winners has to be defended in public ([Post-normal science](../frameworks/1993-post-normal-science.md)).
- [Reversible vs irreversible](reversible-vs-irreversible.md) (logic). One-way doors get the slow, careful process, and with it more scrutiny ([One-way vs two-way doors](../frameworks/2015-one-way-vs-two-way-doors.md)).
- [Direct vs proxy measure](direct-vs-proxy-measure.md) (bundled). Doshi-Velez and Kim say people need explanations when part of the goal, like fairness, safety or the law, never made it into the score ([Need for interpretability](../frameworks/2017-need-for-interpretability.md)).
- [Single objective vs trade-offs](single-objective-vs-trade-offs.md) (study). In Tetlock's hospital study, a manager who spent money on equipment instead of a child's transplant after long thought drew the most outrage of any version ([Taboo trade-offs](../frameworks/1997-taboo-trade-offs.md)). Even weighing a sacred value in public counts against you.
- [Skin in the game vs none](skin-in-the-game-vs-none.md) (independent). Accountable means you must explain the choice, while skin in the game means you pay for it. A judge gives reasons and loses nothing if wrong. A gambler pays for every bad bet and explains nothing.
- [Winnable vs no-win](winnable-vs-no-win.md) (logic). Anyone who must defend a no-win choice will be judged by the loss. A record of what was known at the time is the main defence against [outcome bias](../frameworks/1988-outcome-bias.md).
