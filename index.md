* [Axes](axes/) - Scales with an easy end and a hard end that describe the shape of a problem, in five groups: environment, information, actions, performance and agent.
* [Reading the axes](axes/README.md) - How the axes are grouped, what the correlation labels mean and what changes at the hard end.

* [Frameworks](frameworks/) - Nearly a hundred frameworks, from 1921 to 2024, that sort problems by what they are like rather than by subject.
* [Reading the frameworks](frameworks/README.md) - What the frameworks have in common, how they are ordered and how each one feeds the axes.
